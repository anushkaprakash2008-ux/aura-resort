OUR AMENITIES

At Aura Resort, we offer a wide range of amenities to make your stay truly memorable.

Swimming Pool

Take a refreshing dip in our luxurious infinity pool with stunning ocean views. Open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Spa & Wellness

Rejuvenate your mind and body with our premium spa treatments, including massages, facials, and wellness therapies.

Fitness Center

Stay fit with our state-of-the-art gym equipped with modern cardio and strength training equipment.

Fine Dining

Savor exquisite culinary experiences at our multi-cuisine restaurant with both indoor and outdoor seating options.

Free Wi-Fi

Our dedicated team is available round-the-clock to assist you with any requests or requirements.

Host your special events and celebrations in our elegant banquet halls with premium facilities.

Outdoor Lawn

Beautiful garden setting for intimate celebrations, outdoor events, and private gatherings.
